About DevMonitor

Know what's inside every machine, without the spreadsheet.

DevMonitor is a centralized end-user device monitoring system. Instead of typing serial numbers and specs by hand, you run a lightweight agent on each device. It reads the hardware, lets you review it, and keeps the record up to date in one secure place.

Real hardware, read for you

Processor, cores, memory, storage, graphics, motherboard, operating system and network adapters, collected straight from the device.

You approve everything

The agent shows exactly what it found before anything leaves the device. Nothing is sent until you confirm.

Locked down by default

Accounts are protected by email verification and Google Authenticator. Each device link works once and expires quickly.

One dashboard, every platform

Windows, macOS, Linux and Android devices side by side, with archive and restore when a device is retired.

From download to dashboard in three steps

  1. 01 Download Get the agent for your device from the Downloads page.
  2. 02 Review Open it. It reads the hardware and shows you everything it found.
  3. 03 Link Approve, and the device appears on your dashboard.
